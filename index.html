How to play

This is level 1, played start to finish.

  1. Drag a cord from bulb to bulb

    Put your finger on one bulb and pull the cord to another. The guide line shows where it will go.

  2. Close a triangle around the present

    Three cords make a triangle. Close one around a present and it lights up and unwraps.

  3. Make every cord count

    Each level gives you a handful of cords. Finish with cords to spare and you win three stars.

Light up the city

The level map is now a night city. Every level you clear switches on a little more of it, district by district, until the whole place glows.

    Help us test on Android

    We are a small studio and every tester makes the game better. It takes about two minutes to join.

    1. Sign up

      Fill in the short form with the Google account you use on your Android phone.

      Become a tester

    2. Get added to the test

      We add your account to the closed test and send you the Google Play link.

    3. Install from Google Play

      Open the link on your phone, tap Become a tester, then install Xmas Lights. Already on the list? Open the tester page on Google Play.

    4. Tell us what you find

      Bugs, levels that feel unfair, sounds that annoy you, ideas. All of it helps.
