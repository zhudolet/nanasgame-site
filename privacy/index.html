Privacy Policy

Xmas Lights · Last updated: 25 November 2026

Xmas Lights ("the game") is made by Nana's Game ("we"). This policy explains what data the game handles.

What the game stores

Your progress, stars, Hot Cocoa and settings are saved only on your device. We have no accounts and no servers, and we never receive this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Ads

The free game shows optional video ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, ad interactions, and crash and performance data. Google uses this under its own policies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices

In the EU, UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change it any time under More > Privacy choices. On iPhone you can allow or refuse tracking, and change it in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

This website

nanasgame.com uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. Our host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
