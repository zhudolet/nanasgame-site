Support

Xmas Lights

Stuck on a level, found a bug, or have an idea? Write to contact@nanasgame.com and tell us your phone model and the level number. We read every message.

Common questions

How do I play? Drag a cord from one bulb to another. When three cords close a triangle, it lights up and you get that cord back. Light every goal before you run out of cords.

I ran out of cords. Sip a Hot Cocoa for 3 extra cords. If you have none left, you can watch a short optional video for 3 more, or come back tomorrow for a gift.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. Uninstalling the game removes it.

How do I change my ad choices? Open More > Privacy choices (EU, UK and Switzerland), or your phone's privacy settings. See our privacy policy.
